Five ways groups can be the screen

Same sample people, same balances. Open a direction and say what to keep. The live app currently follows the centered list.

01Centered list

A narrow column. The balance on top, then one row per group. Charts are a single line, not the layout.

 02Always inside a group

Other groups are a strip across the top. The open group fills the center: who pays, then the expenses.

 03Big tiles

Each group is a large card. The amount is the whole point of the tile, and you tap the card to go in.

 04Settle sheet

Home is the list of payments to record. The group name is a label on the line, and Record is the action.

 05Quiet paper

Almost no chrome. A ruled list, a large balance, and the group name beside the amount.
